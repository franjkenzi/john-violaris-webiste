Services

Magistrates' court motoring defence — in depth.

I handle all common motoring offences heard in the magistrates' court, as well as police station representation for road traffic investigations.

Drink & Drug Driving
Complex · High Stakes

Carries a mandatory minimum 12-month disqualification on conviction. I advise on all recognised defences including evidential sufficiency, procedural irregularities, sampling compliance, post-drive consumption, special reasons, and mitigation.

Totting Up — Exceptional Hardship
12+ Points

When a driver accumulates 12 or more penalty points within three years, a mandatory 6-month ban follows unless exceptional hardship is established. This is a high legal threshold — I prepare detailed evidence-based hardship arguments.

Special Reasons Hearings
Specialist

Even where guilt is not in dispute, a special reasons argument can prevent the court from imposing mandatory disqualification or endorsement. Laced drinks, emergency, short distance, misled about insurance.

Speeding Offences
High Volume

NIP validity, s.172 notice requirements, procedural challenges, and representation at any hearing. I advise on every avenue before you accept any fixed penalty or court outcome.

Dangerous & Careless Driving
Trial Work

Dangerous driving carries a mandatory disqualification and potential custody. The distinction between dangerous and careless is a question of degree — and careful analysis of the circumstances is often decisive.

No Insurance & Licence Offences
Regulatory

6–8 mandatory points or discretionary disqualification. Special reasons can avoid endorsement where there was a genuine, reasonable belief that cover was in place.

Mobile Phone, Failing to Stop, s.172
Procedural

All three carry mandatory endorsement with real totting risk. Procedural challenges, NIP validity, and reasonable diligence defences are available in the right cases.

Police Station Representation
Free · Legal Aid

I have represented clients at the police station since 2003 — over 10,000 attendances. Police station advice is free in most cases under legal aid. Early instruction at this stage can transform the outcome of a motoring case.

Not sure which category your case falls into? Call me — I'll assess it free.

Why the Police Station Stage Matters

The police station interview is one of the most consequential stages of any criminal or motoring case. What you say — and what you choose not to say — can determine the entire course of your case. Mistakes made at this stage are often impossible to fully correct later.

What I Do at the Police Station

  • Speak to investigating officers and obtain disclosure before the interview
  • Advise you privately on your position, rights, and options
  • Recommend whether to answer questions, give a prepared statement, or exercise the right to silence
  • Attend the interview with you and intervene where questions are improper
  • Advise on bail or release under investigation conditions

The Advantage of Continuity

One of the most significant benefits of instructing me at the police station stage is continuity. If your case progresses to the magistrates' court, you arrive with a solicitor who already knows your case, your account, and your objectives in full.
